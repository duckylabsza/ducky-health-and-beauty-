The Complete Beginner's Guide to Anti-Ageing Skincare

Published August 2026 · 10 min read

✏️ Research Notes #003

Ageing isn't the enemy.

The beauty industry has spent years convincing us that wrinkles are something to fight, erase or hide.

The truth is a little less dramatic.

Our skin changes as we get older. That's completely normal. Fine lines appear, collagen production slows down, and skin gradually loses some of the bounce it once had. None of that means you've been doing anything wrong.

What does make a difference is how you care for your skin along the way.

The good news is that healthy-looking skin doesn't require a complicated routine or a shelf full of expensive products. A few well-researched ingredients, some consistency, and realistic expectations can go a surprisingly long way.
